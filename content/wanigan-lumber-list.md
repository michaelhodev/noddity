Lumber-only bill of materials for Weston Farmer’s **Wanigan** (~15' garvey / wanigan utility boat). Compiled from the lines/construction drawings and Farmer’s build article. Lengths not printed on the plans are marked **cut to loft/pattern** — loft the boat first, then buy or cut to neat size.

**Out of scope:** fasteners, glue, paint, fiberglass, cable, hardware, and tools.

**Outfitting choice (article):** either the open workboat layout (risers, thwarts, stern sheet) **or** the camping layout (short deck, side decks, hatch / cuddy). Both lumber sets are listed; buy only the set you will build.

| Item | Size / grade | Notes |

| Bow header | **7/8" × 2"** | Oak **or** yellow pine | Cut to loft/pattern |
| Transom cheeks | **3/4" × 3"** | White oak | Extend to floor line if building upside-down |
| Stern / other knees | ~**1-1/8"** thick | Natural crook preferred | Cut to loft/pattern |
| Frames (Fr. 1–4 + forward mold) | ~**1-1/4" × 2"** natural knees | See frame wood below | Handclasped / doubled across the bottom |
| Skeg (optional) | Short piece (~**9"** as drawn) | Same as keel / oak scrap | Dotted on drawings; useful for open-water tracking |

### Frame knee wood (article)

Prefer **natural-crook** flitches from stumps:

- Good: elm, white oak, spruce, apple, lemonwood, yew, cedar, ash
- Avoid: birch, maple (short-lived in this use)

One good stump often yields all frame knees.

## 3. Planking and transom (required hull)

| Item | Size / grade | Species / type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bottom (vee + foreplane bib) | Full **1/2" marine** (or waterproof exterior) plywood | Marine / exterior plywood | Article: **3' × 12'** panel for vee’d bottom and foreplane bib; do not go thinner |
| Topsides (primary) | **Two layers of 1/4" plywood** | Marine / exterior plywood | Inner layer first, then outer layer over it; cut to loft/pattern for ~15' sides |
| Transom | **3/4" 5-ply** plywood | Marine / exterior plywood | Expand from loft (not from body-plan end view alone); allow for rake when beveling |
| Bow cross-planks | **11/16" × 5"** | Spruce **or** pine | Flat noses preferred over cupped round-nose planks |
| Spray whiskers | Small oak strakes (sharp section) | Oak | Port and starboard; break bow-wave cling |

### Topsides alternate (instead of double 1/4" plywood)

Seam-batten planking from full **1/2"** cedar, spruce, mahogany, or other good planking wood. Do **not** substitute single **3/8"** plywood for the sides.

## 6. Yard notes

- Loft first, then make the lumber list from neat sizes on the loft floor (article).
- Take patterns to the yard; short knees, chines, and fashion pieces often come from the scrap pile.
- Pay for full sheets on planking and large members; cut them yourself rather than paying mill waste on tiny pieces.
- Use only species substitutions the drawings or article allow (e.g. keel mahogany **or** yellow pine; bow header oak **or** yellow pine).
- Hull length reference: **15' 0"**; max half-breadth about **31-1/2"** (beam ~**63"**).
